About

Who I Am

Self-taught software developer, systems designer and founder of DeDynamics. Based in Heerenveen, the Netherlands.

I learn by building. When I do not know something, I say so and work it out. When I know something and I am good at it, I do not pretend otherwise.

I am neurodivergent. That is not a marketing angle. It directly affects how I think, work and design software. I care about clear interfaces, persistent context and systems that reduce unnecessary mental work.

I was a photographer before I became a developer, and I still am one. Photography taught me to notice structure, detail and what does not belong in the frame.

Expertise

What I Do

Agentic AI Systems
I design and build distributed agent networks: MCP services, multi-agent coordination, shared tools, session recovery and persistent context across models and machines.

Bionic Persistent Memory
Bionic MaxMem is the most ambitious part of my infrastructure work. It is designed to keep agents oriented across sessions, crashes, workstations and model changes. It is not another chat-history wrapper.

Software Development
TypeScript, JavaScript, React and Node.js. I build frontends, backends, APIs, authentication and deployment as parts of one system, usually on Cloudflare.

Product Development
I build from problems I actually understand. Amethyst Mapper began as a tool for managing my own projects. I used it to plan and develop its public version, and I now use it to build the next versions. It is an integrated mind-mapping platform, not an IDE.
